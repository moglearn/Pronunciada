# Calibration layer: design as built and cross-validation evidence

Generated by `python -m bench.calib.report` from `results/tables/*.json`. Every number here is from TRAINING data: 5-fold speaker-grouped CV on speechocean762 train, leave-one-corpus-out on the l2arctic / epadb train splits, FLEURS validation and FLEURS train. No test split was used for fitting, selection, calibration or thresholds (held-out results: english_vs_azure.md, languages.md).

## 1. Design as built

```
audio ─ fbank ─ ZIPA ─ adapter ─┐
text ─ G2P ─ Reference ─────────┴─ score_reference ─ pronkit.features ─ sound calibrator ─ word calibrator ─ sentence calibrator ─ SentenceResult
```

- **One feature implementation**: `pronkit/features.py` (numpy). `phone_rows` = the base per-phone columns of the feature tables (evidence, span posteriors of the canonical token and its best competitor, log-ratio, entropy, blank, articulatory distance from a shipped token × token panphon table, last-token features, neighbour / word / utterance llr statistics, native z-scores, class, position, nucleus, stress); `derive` = derived columns computed from base columns only; `word_table` / `sentence_table` = the word / sentence calibrator inputs. `bench/pipeline/features.py` now calls `phone_rows` (feature_version 3 adds start/end frames).
- **Models**: scikit-learn HistGradientBoosting (training only), exported to `.npz` (children as int16 offsets, float64 thresholds — float32 thresholds flipped splits, max error 0.54 —, float32 leaves) and evaluated by `pronkit.calibrate.TreeEnsemble`: complete-binary-tree layout, one vectorised step per depth level, NaN handled by per-split column copies (NaN→−inf / NaN→+inf), categorical splits by a 257-entry lookup per split. Small categoricals (class, position, competitor class) are one-hot, identity columns (phone, competitor token) native categorical ("mixed" encoding).
- **Heads** per level: regression of the human score; P(lenient ok) and P(strict ok) = boosted log-odds + a 1-D map (Platt or isotonic, the one with the lower grouped-CV ECE on out-of-fold log-odds); P(strict) ≤ P(lenient) enforced; strictness rule logit P_s = (1−s)·logit P_len + s·logit P_str, s clipped to [−0.5, 1.5].
- **Shipped**: English sounds = `sound_B` (feature set B), every other language = `sound_U` (feature set A, trained on: so762) + its logit bias; word and sentence calibrators are universal (no word identity) and always read the universal model's sound log-odds (English included), the inputs they were trained on.
- Files (KB): {'phone_dist.npz': 13.0, 'sentence.npz': 128.1, 'sound_B.npz': 314.4, 'sound_U.npz': 284.3, 'word.npz': 114.7, 'word_reg.npz': 21.4}; shipped total 875.9 KB (budget 3 MB).

## 9. Tried and dropped

- **400 trees for the probability heads** (lr 0.05): +0.002 CV AUC on A's heads vs 200 trees at lr 0.1, at twice the walker cost; dropped to meet the 5 ms calibrator budget (regression heads keep 400).
- **One-hot phone identity** (instead of native categorical splits): B regression Pearson 0.674 vs 0.684, C 0.700 vs 0.713; dropped. One-hot for the small class / position columns costs nothing (A: 0.6647 vs 0.6653) and halves the walker time, so it was kept for those.
- **float32 split thresholds** in the export: broke parity (max 0.54 on raw log-odds); float64 kept.
- **Omission flag at llr_del < 0** for every sound: fired on 4.9 % of train words with mean human accuracy 7.2 / 10, i.e. mostly not omissions; tightened to < −2 (1.4 %).
- **Wider training mixes** for the universal model (§5): no LOCO gain; not shipped.
- **Word prob × tone / stress / pitch probabilities**: rejected most native zh / ja words (see §6); dropped after the test run (stated in languages.md).
- **Native-categorical encoding of every categorical column**: 2× slower walker for A at equal accuracy; dropped.

## 10. Round 2: word acceptance redesign, sentence strict label, learned prosody combination

**This round was designed AFTER the first held-out evaluation** (the round-1 test tables — word AUC 0.87–0.91, lowest-word 0.63–0.76, Azure swap "right word" — motivated it). All fitting and selection below used train splits and FLEURS validation only; the test sets were then evaluated once more (english_vs_azure.md §6, languages.md §5). §6 above describes round 1; the shipped word / sentence models are now the round-2 ones.

**Word acceptance** = "would a rater accept every sound of this word?". Labels: speechocean762 train lenient = every aligned phone ≥ 1.0 AND word accuracy ≥ 5, strict = every aligned phone ≥ 1.8 AND accuracy ≥ 8; l2arctic / epadb train: any annotated error → not strict-ok; FLEURS validation (120 utterances / language, all 5 kinds): word with a corrupted sound → not ok, untouched words and correct references → ok; synthetic weight 40% per head.
